Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

In short: PLY doesn't collect personal information. There's no account, no advertising network and no tracking. Your progress is stored only on your phone.

This policy explains how the PLY chess puzzle app for iPhone ("PLY", "we", "us") handles information. PLY is made by Jude Franczyk.

What stays on your phone

To work, PLY saves the following on your device only:

  • your puzzle rating and puzzle history (which puzzles you played, results and solve times)
  • your settings (sounds, haptics, appearance, chosen board)
  • boards you've collected

We can't see this information; it's never sent to us. You can erase it any time with Profile → Reset progress, or by deleting the app.

What PLY downloads

PLY occasionally downloads a small public file from plypuzzles.com listing which sponsored boards are available. The request contains no account, device identifier or information about how you use PLY. Like any website, the server that delivers it (GitHub Pages, run by GitHub) may briefly record standard technical details such as your IP address for security and operations; see GitHub's privacy statement. We don't use this to identify or track you.

App updates

When PLY opens, it checks Expo's update service (run by Expo, the company whose tools PLY is built with) for small fixes it can download without a full App Store update. That request includes the app's version, your platform (iOS), and a random ID that the update system creates for your installation of PLY. If PLY ever failed to start, it may also include a short error message. The request contains no account, name, email, location or anything about how you play, and the random ID isn't linked to you. See Expo's privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No advertising networks, analytics services or trackers, and nothing used to track you across apps or websites.
  • No accounts, sign-in, contacts, location, photos, camera or microphone.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.

Sponsored boards

Some boards are presented by sponsors and show their name and logo. Sponsors receive no information about you. If you tap a sponsor's name, their website opens in your browser, and their own privacy policy applies there.

Children

PLY is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes

If PLY ever starts collecting anything beyond what's described here (for example, optional accounts or anonymous counts of how often sponsored boards are seen), we'll update this policy first and change the date above. Anything like that will be described here before it reaches the app.
