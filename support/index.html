Support

Need help with PLY? Email support@plypuzzles.com and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

How is my rating worked out?
PLY uses the Glicko-2 rating system. A clean solve counts as a win, a solve after a hint counts half, and a wrong move, showing the solution or skipping counts as a miss. Quick solves earn a little more. Retries and puzzles you've seen before are never rated.

Is my progress backed up?
Your progress is stored only on your phone, so deleting the app erases it. Optional accounts with backup may come later.

How do I keep a sponsored board?
Featured boards are available for a limited time. Solve the number of puzzles shown in the Themes tab while the board is available and it's yours for good, under Sponsor Boards.

How do I start over?
Profile → Reset progress. Your settings and collected boards are kept.

Where do the puzzles come from?
From the Lichess open puzzle database, released into the public domain (CC0). Thank you, Lichess.
